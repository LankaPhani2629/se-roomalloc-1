How Room Allocation Works

Our transparent 4-stage workflow guarantees zero bias, instant audit trails, and stress-free check-in for every resident.

Code of Conduct

Rules & Regulations

Adherence to the residential handbook ensures a safe, respectful, and academically productive living environment for everyone.

Curfew & Campus Timings
  • Hostel main gates close promptly at 9:00 PM for all residents.
  • Late entry requires prior written approval from the assigned Resident Warden.
  • Overnight leave or home visits must be requested in writing by the student's registered guardian.
Zero Tolerance Policies
  • Ragging is strictly prohibited and punishable under law with immediate rustication.
  • Possession or consumption of alcohol, tobacco, or contraband is strictly banned.
  • Unauthorized electrical appliances (heaters, heavy electric stoves) are not permitted in dorms.
Room Allotment & Vacating
  • A student may possess only one active room allotment across the university.
  • Room swapping without official warden approval and system transfer request is prohibited.
  • Residents must hand over all room inventory in good condition upon vacating.
Fee Payment & Renewal
  • Allotment provisional holds expire automatically if fees are unpaid past the deadline.
  • Fee payment receipt must be preserved and presented upon hostel check-in.
  • All financial dues must be settled to obtain the academic semester no-dues certificate.
