Data Privacy & Protection Policy

Compliance with university policies and international data protection standards (GDPR-aligned).

1. Information We Collect

The Campus Hostel Room Allocation System processes student housing applications, room assignments, attendance records, mess billing, and maintenance complaints. We collect and store:

  • Personal & Identity Information: Student roll number, official name, gender, academic course, year of study, merit score, permanent address, contact telephone, and email.
  • Guardian Details: Emergency contact name and phone number.
  • Housing Records: Hostel block, room number, roommates, check-in dates, and fee receipts.
  • Authentication Data: Usernames, salted cryptographic password hashes, and session access timestamps.
2. Purpose of Processing

Your information is processed exclusively for campus residential logistics, capacity planning, student safety verification, mess billing calculations with meal rebates, and facility maintenance resolution.

3. Data Retention & Security

All student records are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are never stored in plain text and are hashed using industry-standard salted PBKDF2/SHA256 algorithms. Sessions automatically expire after 30 minutes of inactivity.

4. Your Data Rights (Right to Access & Portability)

Students have the right to inspect and download an electronic copy of their personal records at any time. When logged in, visit the Student Data Export endpoint to download your complete profile, allocation, and complaint history in machine-readable JSON format.

5. Contact Information

For data inquiries, rectification requests, or security disclosures, please contact the Chief Warden and University Systems Administrator at hostel-admin@college.edu.
